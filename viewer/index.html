    Flights

    letters, digits, _ and - (accents and emoji are removed)

    How it works

    For Betaflight drones that log to the onboard flash or an SD card (F4 / G4 / F7 / H7 boards). Close Betaflight Configurator first. Alpline finds the drone, switches it to USB drive mode, copies the blackbox to logs/blackbox/ and adds each flight with GPS to the location where it took off (or to this one, with the box ticked). Flights already added are skipped. Unplug the drone afterwards: it stays in drive mode until then. GPS must be logged: in the Configurator's Blackbox tab, check that GPS is among the logged fields.

    Advanced
    What the arrows show

    Speed arrow: where the drone will be in 2 s (at 1×). Acceleration arrow: how hard it speeds up, brakes or turns. Both come from the GPS positions, so very short spikes are smoothed out.

    Battery

    Sag vs current

    What this shows

    Each dot: how far the cell voltage dropped below its resting value at that current. A healthy pack drops little; the slope of the line is the pack's internal resistance. It rises as a pack ages, and it is higher when the pack is cold or nearly empty (orange dots: the last 300 mAh). Resistance needs some punch-outs in the flight to be measured.

    How to sync

    To sync: click Linked to unlink, pause the video on a clear event (props starting at arm, a sharp flip), move the timeline to the same moment in the log, then Sync here. A 2nd point later in the flight also corrects drift.

    Radio setup (EdgeTX, TX15)

    On the radio: SYS → Hardware → USB-VCP = Telemetry mirror (EdgeTX 2.9+; the menu name can differ slightly). Plug the USB cable and choose USB Serial (VCP) when the radio asks. Then [refresh] here, pick the port and [connect], ideally while the drone is still on the ground so its GPS altitude gets calibrated. Warnings are computed from the 3D shadows of this scene ([signal] tab): the path of the next seconds is checked against the height below which there is no line of sight to the pilot. The voice says the reason, then one action: Blind zone ahead, climb 30, No line of sight, turn left, Signal weak, turn back. Left / right are from the drone's direction of flight.
