ALPLINE by lieka · open on your phone

Your phone talks to this computer over the local network: no internet needed at the field.
How
  1. Phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi. At the field: switch on the phone's hotspot and connect the laptop to it.
  2. Scan the code with the phone's camera (or type the address in Chrome and the PIN when asked). The PIN is remembered on the phone.
  3. Once per phone, for the secure (https) page: on the phone open to download alpline-ca.crt, then Android Settings → Security → More security settings → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate and pick it. Until then Chrome shows "Your connection is not private": it still works through Advanced → Proceed, but keeping the screen on and the radio over USB need the certificate.
  4. The first time, Windows may ask whether Python may use the network: allow it. If the phone can't connect, the launcher's phone check fixes the Windows firewall; McAfee or a VPN may need an exception too.
  5. On the phone, Chrome menu → Add to Home screen gives it an icon.

The phone can look, fly the simulator, follow live telemetry and build locations. Deleting and renaming only work on this computer. The radio stays plugged into the computer for live flights. The certificate is made by this PC for this PC only.
