ALPLINE · drone take-off ideas

The antenna (the "legs") always stays out. Plays when the drone leaves the ground (live flight and training). Each one replays by itself every 4 s.

0 · NowWhat the app does today, for comparison: the antenna folds away (to be changed).
1 · Shadow lift-offSeen from above, the drone leaves its shadow on the ground: the shadow slides away, blurs and fades while the drone grows a little, coming up towards you.
2 · Prop washThe propellers become spinning discs, dust rings spread out on the ground under them, then it lifts off.
3 · Antenna linkIt lifts, the antenna flashes twice and the pulse runs from the antenna up the stem into the waves: the link holds as it leaves the ground.
4 · WobbleIt leaves the ground with a small roll and pitch wobble that settles, like a real quad finding its balance.
5 · Punch outFPV style: a hard punch up with speed lines underneath, then it settles back into place.
6 · Full sequenceDiscs spin up, dust rings, the shadow drops away and the antenna sends the pulse up the stem. Everything at once.
