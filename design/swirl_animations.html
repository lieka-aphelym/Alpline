ALPLINE · swirl animation ideas

Only the swirl changes; the arrow keeps what it does now (signal sent / transmitting). The small logo is the header size.

WHEN A PAGE OPENS (ONCE)

0 · Now · spin-upWhat the app does today, for comparison.
1 · Blades fly inThe three blades come in from outside, turning, and lock together into the swirl.
2 · Prop stopSpins fast, then stops like a propeller on the brake: a small overshoot back and a settle.
3 · Motion blurSpins up fast with ghost trails behind each blade; the trails fade as it slows down.
4 · Tilt inThe swirl starts tilted like a prop disc seen from the side and turns flat to face you while spinning.
5 · One by oneEach blade sweeps in around the circle, one after another, then the arrow sends its signal.

WHILE LOADING / BUILDING (LOOP)

0 · Now · steady spinWhat the app does today, for comparison.
1 · ThrottleThe spin speeds up and slows down each turn, like someone working the throttle.
2 · Blade relayThe swirl stays still; the blades light up one after another, so it seems to turn without moving.
3 · Signal ringsSlow spin; a faint ring leaves the swirl every turn, like a signal going out.
4 · BreathingA slow turn while the swirl gently grows and shrinks. The calmest.
5 · Motion blurSteady spin with short ghost trails behind the blades.
