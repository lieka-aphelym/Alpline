ALPLINE · drone icon animations

For the drone schema (concept 7), meant for big places: launch screen, launcher, about box. Opening ones play once; loops are for loading / waiting.

WHEN IT OPENS (ONCE)

A1 · Power-upThe frame draws out from the centre, each motor appears, the propellers spin up and settle, then the signal is sent.
A2 · ArmingLike arming a real quad: the motors twitch one by one in the Betaflight order (1 rear-right, 2 front-right, 3 rear-left, 4 front-left), then all spin up and the signal goes out.
A3 · Take-offThe propellers spin up, the drone lifts a little, the landing legs fold away and the signal is sent.

LOOPS (LOADING / WAITING)

L1 · Props spinningThe four propellers turn, opposite pairs in opposite directions like a real quad; the waves transmit.
L2 · Signal pingThe brackets at each arm move outward and fade, like the signal radiating from the drone; the waves transmit.
L3 · Link scanThe corners light up one after another, clockwise, like checking the link around the drone.
L4 · HoverThe drone floats up and down and rocks very slightly, holding position. Props turning.

THE SAME FIX FOR THE MAIN LOGO (LOADING)
